# Proposed ADR: A verified card tap opens the member's own account view

Status: proposed implementation, 2026-11-18. Interprets the owner's 16/17 Nov journey in the Auth consolidation. If adopted, it supersedes the implementation details in ADR-0003; it does not change the owner's journey or approve hardware operations.

## Decision
- Plan for the already selected NTAG 424 DNA SUN/SDM profile (ADR-0003 and Step 9's accepted tap verification proposal). A plain NDEF URL, static UID or QR can identify a design/demo link, not prove a fresh physical tap: it is copyable. An SDM MAC on fresh counter-bearing data authenticates a tag/read under its provisioned key; it does not prove proximity, possession by the intended member, or that a harvested but unused URL wasn't relayed. Require the member's own session as a separate authority. Do not fall back to UID-only access.
- NDEF URL: `https://sophomores.co/nfc/<public-card-ref>?picc=<SDM encrypted payload>&cmac=<SDM MAC>`. The ref is random and public, never the UID, member ID, primary email, secret, or an authorization token. Provisioning fixes byte encoding, offsets, exact lengths and test vectors; the verifier authenticates the complete approved profile and cross-checks the immutable chip identity for that ref. Query fields must each appear once; clients cannot supply routing, identity or counter separately. No production tag is written in this slice.
- `GET /nfc/[id]` is a private, uncached server ingress, never linked or prefetched by the site. Decline explicitly marked speculative requests. Resolve the fresh canonical member principal, verify SDM, and authorize the pre-bound same account. On a customer mismatch, revoked card, malformed proof or replay, show a clean generic tap-unavailable view; expose no existence signal, identity or reason. Signed-out users see generic sign-in instructions and re-tap after sign-in; the first release doesn't carry proof across a social callback, cookie silo or device. This avoids consuming a counter on anonymous link scanners. Never render or log the proof; redact path/query at ingress, disable analytics on the endpoint, and use private/no-store and no-referrer before a 303 to a clean destination.
- Only for a verified same-account navigation, a single transaction checks the card status and binding again, compare-and-advances its monotonic counter, and classifies first vs later tap; stale/equal counters lose, including concurrent submissions. The transaction may record replay and first-routing bookkeeping, but GET never binds a card, performs an entitlement or wallet mutation, or checks anyone in. The account's first accepted tap goes to `/welcome`; a later verified tap goes to a dedicated membership view using fresh own-account state, with no additional phone gate. A refreshed clean destination is an ordinary authenticated page, not a new tap. First accepted tap records routing, not a guarantee that the device rendered it; keep a direct welcome link for recovery. A replacement card uses the existing account marker and does not begin a new first-tap journey.
- The explicit issuance/bind command is a separate, staff-authorized Payload transaction after the members' distribution record is verified. Stage unpredictable public refs before shipment, program and verify the approved tag profile in the custody station, and bind the expected authenticated account from the fulfillment record before delivery. No first-presenter/self-claim by card proof alone. Enforce one active binding per card and member for the launch cohort; record issue/revoke/replace audit events, retire old cards on replacement, preserve old replay counters, and never reassign retired inventory silently. Keep master material behind the chosen secret boundary and separate from all wallet/Unlock keys; use per-tag diversified, versioned keys. Exact station/operator and live issuance are external admission items, not choices that block this source skeleton.

## GET consumption and its limits

The tap currently proposes a GET so the phone's ordinary NFC browser navigation can reach the right page without a second gesture. An accepted GET is **not side-effect free**: it spends proof and can claim first-welcome routing. Private/no-store, no prefetch links, speculative-header rejection and the fresh same-account session protect against common crawlers and anonymous unfurls; they cannot distinguish every authenticated speculative request, copied-but-unused link, retry, or redirect that never reaches the screen. Do not put tap URLs in any clickable page, analytics stream, or unfurlable content. If an authenticated speculative request spends the URL or redirect delivery fails, mint nothing: let the member open welcome directly or physically re-tap. If platform evidence shows meaningful unintended consumption, prefer a private, non-consuming GET with a deliberate same-origin, CSRF-protected POST to spend proof. That costs an extra gesture and is not implemented or approved here. The clean redirected GET must never spend proof.

## Persistence invariants

- Keep the one-time marker unique on the immutable member account ID (not email, device, card, key, NFC counter or browser session). In the same transaction that advances the replay fence, the first accepted tap with no marker creates it and routes to welcome. Later accepted taps route to membership. The marker records the routing decision, not a successful render or passkey creation. Direct welcome reads and rejected/stale/foreign/anonymous taps never create it.
- Keep a high-water counter per immutable physical card and authenticated key epoch. Compare strictly greater and advance under the same transaction; no wrap or reset within an epoch. Re-resolve the active binding and expected authenticated epoch at commit so proof verified just before revoke/rekey cannot win afterward. Marker and counter changes either both commit or neither does. The existing source skeleton does not carry a key epoch; a concrete verifier/store contract must before integration.
- Losing, revoking, replacing or rekeying a card does not delete/reset the account's welcome marker. Retire the old binding/epoch and retain its counter; accept no proof for a retired card or epoch, even if the counter is higher. A replacement card or newly provisioned cryptographic epoch owns its own counter, never inherited or reset under the same epoch. Activate the replacement only after invalidating the prior binding/epoch; at most one active card for the account and one active epoch for the card. Neither a stale proof nor discovery of a lost card is a recovery or reassignment path.

## Scope and gates

The wallet-activation branch contains welcome UX, not a tap resolver. The door-check-in branch's `door/qr.ts` verifies Unlock's EIP-191 event QR, not SDM; reuse neither its parser nor its scanner authorization. The door credential remains the event Ticket QR scanned by staff in Unlock EVENTS. A passkey is optional sign-in, not Safe ownership; payment settlement and entitlements remain independent.

Add the eventual card, binding and replay records under Payload with an admitted native migration. Wire no production verifier/store/route before vendor vectors, explicit issuer workflow review, concurrent replay and wrong-owner tests, sanitized ingress review, physical tap captures across iOS/Android, and separate guarded admission. The companion code supplies only the parse/auth/atomic-adapter contract and generic outcomes; no fake verifier, device record, route registration or write is included.
