# Footer field: R3F reimplementation — 2026-10-25

> The initial acceptance below missed the scrolling chapter boundary and was insufficient. Its timer-based scheduling and fixed-footer integration are superseded by the pause-only follow-up at the end of this file.

## Scope

Implementation/behavior replacement, not visual redesign. Footer and durable shader editor now share `components/flame-backdrop/field/FieldCanvas.tsx`: R3F Canvas, declarative plane/ShaderMaterial, Three uniforms and texture resources. The original hero and historical study runtime are unchanged. Approved mask, palette, breathing material and wordmarks are unchanged.

- Demand rendering with a 30fps upper-budget timer; no second renderer, RAF loop, camera or resize observer in the shared field.
- Hidden document, explicit pause and inactive footer suspend active time. Loading/failed masks stop continuous drawing. Resuming discards suspended wall time.
- Resize updates the portrait-width uniform; shader source is stable across aspect changes.
- Footer is first mounted on entering contacts, retained afterwards, and hidden/paused away from contacts.
- Mask loads remain alpha-only, race-safe, transparent until ready and independently recoverable. Fatal GPU failures cannot be cleared by a successful mask load.
- Important pointer-events utility overrides R3F's inline auto setting; canvas cannot capture input.

## Follow-up: pause only, with 25vh prewarm

User correction: the footer must not be hidden when navigation changes chapter. Pause the renderer instead, and start it roughly 25vh before the footer enters view.

### Root cause and correction

- Reproduced the defect on the supplied local preview: at 529x812, contacts top was 363.7px (still visibly occupying the viewport), but the shared fixed backdrop opacity was 0 and field display was none. This was a navigation-state cut, not a shader problem.
- The fixed backdrop is now hero-only. The footer field belongs to the actual invitation section, in an absolute clipped holder with a sticky viewport-height surface. It remains mounted and display-visible; normal scrolling carries/clips the section, without nav-controlled hiding or a new fade.
- `useFooterPlayback` uses Motion's viewport-axis offsets `start 125%` / `end -25%` solely to switch GPU activity. No IO percentage root-margin approximation: IO vertical percentages resolve against width, not height.
- R3F has a single frameloop authority: Canvas receives `always` when requested active and the material/mask is available, otherwise `demand`. The custom timeout loop is removed. Demand rendering retains the last frame and permits a single mask/resize redraw without advancing time.
- Mask/fatal state controls availability independently. Incidental parent renders cannot override a competing imperative frameloop setter.

### Observed live regression evidence

- Captured 265 frames each for native upward and downward wheel scrolling through the old 40%-viewport navigation boundary. Zero hidden frames and zero demand-mode frames while the footer remained visible. The canvas's top followed the section continuously instead of vanishing when the nav changed to The People.
- At 812px viewport height, the renderer remained `always` with contacts top at 860.5, 925.1, 967.8 and 996.9px: already running before actual entry.
- It switched to `demand` at top 1018.3px, approximately the 1015px threshold (1.25 x 812). The final 100 offscreen samples had identical elapsed time 67.832500.
- On re-entry, the first sampled `always` frame was at top 989.7px, while contacts was still below the 812px viewport. Elapsed resumed from 103.132600, without reset, and advanced before the section became visible.
- Evidence: `artifacts/flame-r3f-20261025/scroll-pause/mobile-frames.json` and `footer-boundary-mobile.jpg`.
- Browser changes during separate observations can move scroll position; freeze assertions above use position-verified samples from a single continuous trace rather than assuming the user's tab stayed untouched between calls.
