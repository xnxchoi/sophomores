[TITLE]
Cloth and foil

Cypress cloth over board, no jacket. Title and mark blind-debossed; brass foil on the debossed lines is the highlight layer, the way Stripe's book catches light on its printed lines.

[TITLE]
Vellum jacket

A translucent vellum jacket over an oxblood or porphyry board. The board's colour shows through. In 3D the jacket can lift away: a second interaction on the book page.

[TITLE]
Quiet cover, loud edges

A plain Ink cover; the page edges sprayed terracotta or brass. The colour appears only as the book turns, which suits a scroll sequence.
